Official Rules

01 · TIME ATTACK QUALIFYING · 23 OCT – 28 NOV

All attempts must take place at MAIDA Gaming Hub. Repeated attempts are allowed during the qualifying period. Only each driver's single fastest valid lap counts. Eight fastest advance.

02 · TOP 8 SHOOTOUT · 29 NOV

Eight qualifying drivers record new times. Four fastest advance. Times do not carry over.

03 · TOP 4 SUPER POLE · 29 NOV

Four drivers race again. Third place receives RM100 and fourth place RM50. Two fastest advance to the Grand Final.

04 · THE ELITE · GRAND FINAL · 29 NOV

Two finalists race for the title. Fastest wins RM300; runner-up wins RM150. Previous lap times do not carry over.
